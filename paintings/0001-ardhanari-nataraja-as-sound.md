## Caption

Father and Mother, Warrior and Nurturer.
His trident holds the three — wanting, knowing, doing.
Her fire is antaragni — the wanting that lights the ring.

Sound and Light, Vibration and Energy.
Shiva's damru beats, and Shakti's heart beats in unison.

She nurtures the sapt sindhu of life.

## Decisions made while making it

- Two legs, each in its own half; nothing crosses the seam. The earlier idea of Shiva's leg crossing
  into Shakti's half was refused.
- Feet swapped from the canon: Shakti's foot on Apasmāra, Shiva's in the air.
- Shiva is not silence. His drum beats; her heart beats; they are in unison. The reading "the silence
  and the sound it makes" was refused as contradicting that.
- Register of the caption: paired nouns, one image per line, nothing explained.
