# Paintings — the aṣṭaka

Eight panels: one per stream, and an eighth for the confluence. *Aṣṭaka* — a set of eight, and
also an octet: seven notes and the octave, which is the first note again, one level up.

Each panel carries three layers: the **source image** from the bank's own text, one **Naṭarāja
element**, and one **family episode** (Shiva, Shakti, Gaṇeśa, Kārttikeya). Names are earned from
the text or the icon, or marked as assigned. Accepted 2026-09-13, provisionally.

## Rules

- A painting is the operator's. Concept, sketch, caption: first-hand. A render from a sketch is a
  study, and says so.
- The caption is filed verbatim. It is not edited after filing; a change is a new version.
- The seven svaras and their animals (`sources/STREAMS.md`, when landed) are the fable cast; the
  paintings take their figures from the layers above.
