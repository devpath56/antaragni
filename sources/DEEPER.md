## Two things to carry, not read

**Śiva Sūtra 3.9 — *nartaka ātmā*.** The Self is the dancer. Not Śiva dancing for you; you, on the
inner stage, with the senses as audience. The line that separates Trika's picture from Chidambaram's.

**Śiva's five powers — *cit, ānanda, icchā, jñāna, kriyā*.** Consciousness, bliss, will, knowledge,
action. *Icchā* is a power, not an attachment; wanting to win is a śakti. *Kriyā* is why the self is
*kartṛ* — doer — before it is knower. The five *kañcukas* are these powers contracted, which is what
makes them a taxonomy of exceptions.
