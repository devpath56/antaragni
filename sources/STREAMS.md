# The seven streams

Seven streams of thought. Each steered by two sources that disagree.

**Sindhu is the confluence, not a tributary** — the main stem is the person the seven feed.

Accepted 2026-09-12, provisionally. Pairs are **symmetric**: neither bank is the source and neither
is the corrective. They disagree, and the disagreement locates the boundary that neither would have
marked alone.

| # | stream | Eastern | Western | what they fight about |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **power** | Tiruvaḷḷuvar, *Tirukkuṟaḷ* — *Poruṭpāl* | Robert Caro, *The Power Broker* | virtue binds power · or power reveals what constraint was hiding |
| 2 | **authority** | Kabir — frame-break dohas | Nassim Taleb | inherited form is dead weight · or it carries information you cannot see |
| 3 | **contest** | Viṣṇuśarman, *Pañcatantra* | Thomas Schelling, *The Strategy of Conflict* | win by deception · or by binding yourself so credibly you cannot retreat |
| 4 | **moving people** | Bharata, *Nāṭyaśāstra* | Edward Bernays, *Propaganda* | universalise so an audience shares the feeling · or manufacture consent against their interest |
| 5 | **striving** | Zeami, *Fūshikaden* | Friedrich Nietzsche | follow a staged form transmitted by a master · or overcome every form you were handed |
| 6 | **Sarasvatī** — reasoning | Gautama, *Nyāya Sūtra* | Karl Popper | establish truth by valid means · or never confirm anything, only fail to refute |
| 7 | **generating** | Pāṇini, *Aṣṭādhyāyī* | Kurt Gödel | a finite rule set can generate everything · or completeness has a price no system escapes |

## Why Sarasvatī, and only Sarasvatī

The one river name earned rather than assigned. Goddess of speech and learning, which maps to the
stream directly — and the river that **vanished into desert sand**.

The governing clause of the source poem reads: *where the clear stream of reason has not lost its
way into the dreary desert sand of dead habit.* Tagore put the stream and the desert into the same
line, which is the exact fate of the seventh river.

So the reasoning stream carries its own warning in its name. This is the one that can disappear, and
dead habit is how. The remaining six rivers carry no semantics that could be mapped honestly, and
assigning them anyway would repeat the mistake that killed the rishi framing.

## Open on row 1

**Caro over Machiavelli**, and this was chosen rather than instructed.

Machiavelli is the cleaner doctrinal collision — *a prince must learn how not to be good* against
*wealth without honour should be cast off* — but 1513 strains the modern-Western rule, and taking
him means granting a periodization exception on the operator's behalf.

Caro satisfies the rule without one, and the collision is real though different in kind: Valluvar
prescribes how power should be held; Caro documents how it is actually taken, by whom, and what it
exposes. *Power doesn't corrupt. Power reveals.*

Machiavelli is on the bench and one word restores him.

## Rule

**Both banks, or the stream stays shut.** A stream is not open until both sources are read.
Otherwise the result is fourteen half-read sources and no cases.

Recommended first stream: **1**. *Poruṭpāl* is already scoped, *The Power Broker* is a single
complete unit, and it is the seat actually occupied — advisor to power.
