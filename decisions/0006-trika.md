# 0006 — Trika over Siddhānta, and the agency lens on all seven streams

Settled 2026-09-12. Operator ruling: "I lean Trika, reject Siddhānta."

## The ruling

| decision | why logical | assumption | falsified if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trika (Kashmir non-dual Śaivism) over Śaiva Siddhānta as the metaphysics under stream 4 | the self as agent — *kartari jñātari svātmani*, Utpaladeva's opening — is the only ground on which a high-agency casebook is coherent. Siddhānta's grace-dependence and Advaita's witness-self both fail the gate. In Trika desire is a power (*icchā-śakti*), not an attachment to shed | agency is a property of consciousness, not something granted to it | a period of acting *as if* grace-dependent produces better decisions than acting as sovereign |

What it changes about Naṭarāja: the Chidambaram bronze is Siddhānta's picture — Śiva dances *for*
the devotee, who watches and receives grace. Trika's picture is *Śiva Sūtra* 3.9–11: the Self is
the dancer, the inner self the stage, the senses the spectators. Under Trika the vīra → adbhuta
arrow keeps its mechanism and gains its concept: the witness's wonder is a flash of recognition of
their own uncontracted agency, so the break condition *the witness could have done it* changes sign
— the wonder works *because* they could. Raudra and vīra become one śakti at two apertures,
*saṅkoca* and *vikāsa*.

## The seven, re-read through agency

One question per bank: does it treat the agent as the source of outcomes, or as subject to
something outside it?

| # | stream | Eastern — agency read | Western — agency read | the fight, agency-framed | change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | power | the king makes the kingdom; virtue is chosen | **power reveals** — remove constraint and what the agent was manifests | Trika: unconstrained agency reveals Śiva. Caro: it revealed Moses | **Caro ratified.** Sharper against Trika than Machiavelli, who is about choice under necessity |
| 2 | authority | realisation overrides inherited form | time's filter outranks the agent's judgement | is time (*kāla*) an authority over the agent, or a self-imposed limit? | framing sharpened |
| 3 | contest | the limited agent wins by wit — *vidyā* over *kalā* | win by removing your own options | is freedom power, or is credible self-binding power? | none — now the sharpest collision in the corpus |
| 4 | moving people | rasa arises only in the *sahṛdaya*; the moved party is an agent | the audience is herd; the moved party is an object | is the audience an agent? | **Bernays ratified for the stream.** Heuristic: create sahṛdayas, do not manage a mass — fails on non-sahṛdaya audiences, which is Bernays's ground |
| 5 | striving | technique, staged form, a master — *āṇavopāya* | self-overcoming, values from oneself — nearest Western thing to *svātantrya* | Trika's own ranking of means, applied to striving | **Zarathustra ratified** over *Genealogy*. The one stream where the Western bank is the more agentic |
| 6 | reasoning | the knower establishes truth by valid means | the agent conjectures; reality decides | does the agent ever get to know? | none. Nyāya's metaphysics is realist-pluralist but the scope is the adversarial catalogue, which Utpaladeva himself used |
| 7 | generating | a finite rule set generates the infinite | the system cannot reach all its truths — but the mathematician can, from outside | the agent exceeds its own generator | **Gödel ratified**; the earlier "wrong theorem" objection withdrawn. Kātyāyana is the Eastern *evidence* for the claim, not a rival bank |

Nothing on either bank fails the gate.

## Two tools the ruling supplies

**Type every exception by its kañcuka.** Six of seven pairs share a shape — the Eastern bank asserts
the agent's power, the Western bank names a limit — and Trika's five *kañcukas* are a taxonomy of
limits on agency: *kalā* (capacity), *vidyā* (knowledge), *rāga* (desire), *kāla* (time), *niyati*
(constraint). Proposed, not textual:

| stream | the Western bank names | kañcuka |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | what appetite does when constraint goes | *rāga* |
| 2 | time as judge | *kāla* |
| 3 | voluntary limitation of one's own power | *kalā* |
| 4 | the audience not knowing it is moved | *vidyā* |
| 6 | the knower never fully knowing | *vidyā* |
| 7 | the system bound by its own rules | *niyati* |
| 5 | reversed — Zeami's form is the *niyati*; Nietzsche overcomes it | — |

A heuristic that fails on *kāla* fails differently from one that fails on *vidyā*. The fourth field
becomes queryable. This does not overturn the symmetric-pairs ruling; direction is still not a rule.

**Grade every heuristic by its upāya.** Technique (*āṇava*), reframe (*śākta*), or act of will
(*śāmbhava*). Stream 5's whole fight is this grading.
