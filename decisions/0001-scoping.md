# 0001 — Source scoping

Settled 2026-09-12.

## Settled

| call | decision | reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Valluvar — *Aṟattuppāl* | **spine, not body** — householder, gratitude, non-killing | dropping virtue entirely and pairing Valluvar's statecraft with the Pañcatantra's how-to-win rebuilds Kauṭilya, which was rejected on purpose |
| Valluvar — *Kāmattuppāl* | **second half** (*Kaṟpiyal*, wedded love), read non-literally | sustained relationship as the domain, not courtship; a proxy for reading another person over time |
| Form restriction | **relaxed** — verse for heuristics, prose and narrative for concept and exceptions | the original verse-only rule is incompatible with the quartet |
| Bharata | **both** effect theory and construction craft | why people are moved, and how to build the thing that moves them |
| Abhinavagupta | **apparatus layer**, not a scored source | his load-bearing work is prose commentary; he explains the other sources rather than adding a voice |
| Retrieval indexing | **post-synthesis**, not a design input | index after the raw material is synthesized and scoped, not before |

## Chosen after pricing

- **Nyāya scope: the full adversarial catalogue.** Means of valid knowledge, plus the fallacies of
  the stated reason, plus deliberate equivocation, the twenty-four hollow rejoinders and the
  twenty-two grounds of defeat. Chosen over the narrower tiers because it is the only one that helps
  in a live room with another person, and because each named move is already quartet-shaped:
  situation, why it is void, and what it still permits.

## Reversed

- **Kauṭilya admitted, then eliminated.** Looks like an obvious fit and is a trap — spies, forts,
  alliances, assessment of strength is *Poruṭpāl* again in Sanskrit prose. Its only marginal
  contribution over Valluvar is removing the moral constraint, which was already a settled choice.

- **Nyāya eliminated, then restored.** First cut as redundant with Valluvar's judgment. That
  conflated *practical* judgment — assess strength, time, ground — with *formal* epistemology.
  Valluvar has no theory of inference or evidence at all.

- **"Inspiring fables on how to win" reassigned.** Kauṭilya wrote no fables; the *Arthaśāstra* is
  prose sūtra about tax schedules and superintendents. The fable tradition is *nīti* literature —
  the Pañcatantra, and behind it the Jātakas and a common stock of oral tales whose direction of
  borrowing is unresolved.
