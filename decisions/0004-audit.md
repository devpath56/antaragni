# 0004 — Decision audit

Every load-bearing call made while scoping this corpus, with the assumption it rests on and the
evidence that would overturn it. A decision with no falsifier is a preference.

| # | decision | why logical | assumption | falsified if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Valluvar as the anchor, not Kabir | Kabir's fearlessness requires having nothing to lose; the anchor must survive having dependents, property and obligations | high agency is exercised *within* commitments, not by shedding them | a period of holding nothing turns out to produce better decisions than a period of holding much |
| 2 | Case quartet as the unit | a heuristic without its reason cannot be bounded, and one without its boundary gets applied where it is false | misapplication, not ignorance, is the dominant failure mode | cases with exceptions are applied no better than bare heuristics |
| 3 | Exceptions are mandatory | the rarest field in aphorism literature and the only one that prevents cargo-culting | boundary conditions are knowable in advance for most heuristics | most exceptions turn out discoverable only after the failure |
| 4 | *Aṟattuppāl* kept as spine | dropping virtue and pairing statecraft with how-to-win rebuilds Kauṭilya, who was rejected deliberately | the moral constraint is load-bearing rather than decorative | the constraint never once changes an actual decision |
| 5 | Verse for heuristics, prose admitted for concept and exceptions | rules compress into verse; reasons and boundaries live in commentary and story | the four fields have different natural forms | commentary turns out to add no exceptions the verse did not already carry |
| 6 | Nyāya at the full adversarial tier | the only tier that helps in a live room; each named move is already quartet-shaped | the hard situations are adversarial, not solitary | the catalogue is never once reached for in an actual dispute |
| 7 | Transfer cost removed as a criterion | it measured downstream work, not the source's knowledge; it would have cut the *Arthaśāstra* for naming Mauryan tax rates | particularity is work undone, not knowledge absent | universalising a particular source proves impossible rather than merely costly |
| 8 | Popularity is **not** a disqualifier | the Tirukkuṟaḷ is more widely distributed in Tamil Nadu than the Gītā is in India; a popularity test cuts the anchor first | wide readership is uncorrelated with depth | mass-held sources are found to be systematically shallower on inspection |
| 9 | A source must ask a question still open | the Gītā spends its length on *should I act at all*, which is closed here | the question is genuinely settled and will stay settled | a period arrives when the motive to act is actually in doubt |
| 10 | Bhagavad Gītā rejected | its doctrine is action without attachment to fruit; the operator's position is intense attachment held on a longer horizon | detachment removes the engine rather than steadying it | Tilak's reading holds — that the text releases anxiety about results, not the goal |
| 11 | Sapta sindhu over sapta rishi | rivers converge and have sources and confluences; seven biographies do not, and mapping sources onto named rishis was arbitrary in four of seven cases | structure should carry meaning, not decoration | a rishi mapping is found that is principled rather than forced |
| 12 | Paired banks per stream | exception density was the scarcest criterion; a counterpart generates the field structurally instead of waiting for a source to volunteer its own limits | genuine disagreement between two sources locates the boundary | pairs are found to talk past each other rather than collide |
| 13 | Both banks or the stream stays shut | otherwise the result is fourteen half-read sources and no cases | reading breadth without depth produces nothing usable | partial reading of many sources yields more cases than complete reading of few |
| 14 | High-agency test on the second bank | a source explaining why you do not control outcomes cannot serve a casebook about acting | agency is a precondition for a decision case, not a conclusion from one | a low-agency source produces usable decision cases anyway |
| 15 | Sarasvatī names the reasoning stream | the only river name carrying semantics — speech and learning — and the only river that vanished into desert sand, which is the image in the governing clause of the source poem | a name should be earned rather than assigned | the etymology or the geography turns out not to support it |
| 16 | Euclid cut, Gödel in | Euclid and Pāṇini both build generative systems from compact rule sets: complementary, so the pair produces no exceptions | a second bank exists to disagree, not to echo | a genuine opposition between the two is found |
| 17 | Rejections carry the signal | anyone can agree with Valluvar; what is refused, and on what date, is specific to one person, and the sequence records character over time | acceptance is common and refusal is discriminating | the rejections turn out to be as generic as the acceptances |
| 18 | First-hand quartets are operator-authored only | a case filed as lived experience and written by someone else is false at the citation, whatever its content | provenance is part of a case's truth | — not falsifiable; this is a rule about honesty, not an empirical claim |
| 19 | Public repo as a filter | differentiation by rigour repels the incurious and attracts the few who find it interesting | the intended reader is rare and identifiable by what they stop on | the repo attracts volume rather than fit, or nobody at all |
| 20 | Corpus-as-code, MIT | quartets become queryable structure; version control supplies the character time-series for free | the collection will be executed against, not only read | it stays prose and the code layer never arrives |
