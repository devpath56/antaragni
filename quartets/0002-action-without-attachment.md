# 0002 — Continuing when results are not coming

**Seat:** striving · **Source:** Bhagavad Gītā, 2.47 and the karma-yoga material
**Verdict: REJECTED, 2026-09-12**

## Situation

You need a reason to keep acting through a long stretch where the results are not arriving.

## Heuristic, as the source states it

Your claim is on the action alone, never on its fruit. Act fully; do not let the result be the
motive.

## Concept, as the source argues it

Attachment to outcome is what produces paralysis — Arjuna is frozen on the field precisely because
he is calculating consequences. Release the grip on the fruit and the action becomes available
again. The agent is steadied because no single result can destabilise him.

## Exceptions the source itself implies

Aimed at someone frozen *before* acting. It is a doctrine for entering, not for sustaining.

## Verdict — rejected

**Reason, in the operator's own words.** "I want to win, that's why I fight. No confusion about
that. High agency is a hard criteria. In TKD I fought and lost so that one day I will be stronger
when I am as old as them and win tournaments because of my training. It's not action without
attachment, quite the opposite. Intense attachment to my goals."

Detachment removes the engine and leaves the discipline with nothing driving it.

**What is granted.** The reading being rejected is the common one. Tilak's *Gītā Rahasya* argues
karma-yoga is a doctrine of energetic action and attacks the quietist reading directly. On that
reading the text releases *anxiety about results*, not the goal — which is much closer to `0001`
than to what is refused here.

**What would reopen this.** Reading Tilak directly and finding that the text supports holding a
terminal goal intensely while refusing to be destabilised by any single intermediate result. That
would not be the heuristic as stated above; it would be a different one, and it would be admitted
under its own number.

**Kept rather than deleted** because the most famous statement of a position is worth holding on to
precisely when you hold the opposite. The rejection is more informative than the admission would
have been.
