# Criteria — v0

What a source must yield to be admitted, and what it is then scored on. Each carries its reason,
because a criterion you can't justify is one you can't apply consistently.

## Gates — pass/fail

A source failing any one of these is out, regardless of how well it scores below.

### Quartet-completable
Yields all four fields, from the text plus its commentary tradition.

**Why:** the unit has four fields. A source that can only fill one isn't a source, it's a quotation.

### Situation-indexed
Organized by, or decomposable into, namable moments rather than abstract topics.

**Why:** this casebook is queried situationally, under pressure. A source organized by theme cannot
be reached from inside a situation.

### Authority
A stable text with reliable numbering, and a commentary tradition that argued about it.

**Why:** two reasons. Stable numbering means a case can be cited twice and mean the same thing. A
contested text matters more — a text nobody ever pushed on has no exceptions recorded, because
nobody ever found its edges.

## Scored

### Exception density
Does the source mark its own boundaries?

**Why:** rarest field by far, and the one that prevents misapplication. Most aphorism literature has
none at all.

### Concept depth
Does it state why, or only what?

**Why:** a heuristic you can't justify is a heuristic you can't bound.

### Commentary availability
Is there a living tradition of commentary on it?

**Why:** concept and exceptions come substantially from commentary rather than from the primary
verse. A source with no commentary tradition can hand over heuristics and nothing else.

### Domain overlap
Against the seven situations this casebook exists to serve: holding power well, sustained
intimacy, winning contests, moving people, designing generators, reasoning under adversaries,
and enduring repeated defeat.

**Why:** coverage of a tradition is not the goal.

## Form

Verse for heuristics. Prose and narrative admitted for concept and exceptions.

**Why:** an earlier version of this restricted the corpus to verse and numbered aphorism, to keep
units cleanly taggable. That rule is incompatible with the quartet — couplets compress *rules*,
while the reasons and the boundary conditions live in commentary and in story. The restriction
survives where it was actually load-bearing: anything scored as a heuristic is verse.

## Removed

### Transfer cost — cut from the criteria
Originally scored: is the source already stripped of ownership markers, or must I strip them?

**Why removed:** it measured my downstream work, not the source's knowledge. Scoping by it would
have excluded the *Arthaśāstra* for naming Mauryan tax rates — but particularity is work undone,
not knowledge absent.

Universalization is a processing layer applied to material after selection. It is not a filter on
what material gets in. It now sits in the pipeline, not the gate.
